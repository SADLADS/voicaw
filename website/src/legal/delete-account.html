You can delete your Voicaw account yourself, at any time, from the app or from any browser. It happens at once and can't be undone.

How

  1. Open Voicaw: the app, or app.voicaw.com in a browser, and log in.
  2. Go to Settings → My Account → Delete Account.
  3. If you own guilds, either make someone else their owner first, or tick the box to delete them with your account.
  4. Enter your password and choose Delete my account.

Can't log in any more (no password and no recovery code)? Write to {{CONTACT}} with your username. Since nobody can check your password for you, you may be asked for things only the account's owner would know before anything is deleted.

What's deleted

  • Your profile: display name, status, bio and picture. Your username is freed for others.
  • Everything needed to log in: password data, the copies of your encryption keys, your recovery code data, and every device you were logged in on (all are logged out).
  • Your notes, settings, spellcheck dictionary, favourite GIFs, muted and pinned conversations, and notification subscriptions.
  • Your place in every guild and group chat (and which of their events you were interested in); guilds you own, with everything in them, if you chose to delete them.
  • Bot applications you made: each bot leaves every guild and stops working (its messages stay, from a deleted bot).

What stays

Messages are part of other people's conversations, so they stay where they are, shown as from "Deleted User":

  • Messages you sent in direct messages, group chats and guilds, with your reactions, and the records of calls you were in. (Those in direct messages and group chats stay end-to-end encrypted: Voicaw can't read them.)
  • Things you made in guilds: tasks, comments, documents, scheduled events, webhooks, and the guild's audit log entries naming you.
  • An empty placeholder for your account (its number, when it was made and last online, and its public keys), so your old messages still show as from one deleted person.

To remove a particular message, delete it in the app before deleting your account. For anything else, write to {{CONTACT}}. See also the privacy policy.
