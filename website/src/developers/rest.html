Every endpoint below is at https://api.voicaw.com/api/v10, takes and gives Discord's objects, and needs Authorization: Bot <token>. "Needs" is the permission the bot must have there. Like everyone's on Voicaw, the bot's power only reaches below its own highest role: it can't manage roles at or above it, or members ranked as high as it is, and never the guild's owner. Anything not listed answers 404.

Sending messages

JSON, or multipart/form-data with payload_json and files as files[n]. A message needs content, an embed or a file.

  • content: the text, in Discord's markdown. Mentions are written as on Discord: <@user>, <@&role>, <#channel>, @everyone and @here (those two, and roles that aren't mentionable, need Mention Everyone). Custom emoji: <:name:id>.
  • embeds: up to 10, with Discord's limits (6000 characters in all, title 256, description 4096, 25 fields…). attachment://name points at a file sent with the message. Images in embeds load from their own sites in readers' apps.
    • author.icon_size and footer.icon_size (Voicaw's own, not Discord's): the icon's size in pixels, a whole number from 12 to 128. Without it the author's icon is 24 px and the footer's 20 px, as on Discord. Out of range is a 50035 error; it only counts with an icon_url, and comes back in the message's embeds. Libraries that check embeds' fields may need it added by hand (see Differences).
  • Files: up to 10, 25 MB together, stored as sent (guilds aren't encrypted). attachments can give each a filename and description.
  • message_reference: a reply, to a message in the same channel. fail_if_not_exists: false sends it without the reply if that message is gone. Forwarding (type: 1) isn't supported.
  • allowed_mentions: parse, users, roles as on Discord.
  • flags: SUPPRESS_EMBEDS (4) and SUPPRESS_NOTIFICATIONS (4096, no notifications).
  • components: buttons and selects (see Components). A message can be only components.
  • nonce is given back. tts, sticker_ids, poll and enforce_nonce are ignored.

Scheduled events

As Discord's, with voice channel (entity_type 2) and somewhere-else (3) events; Voicaw has no stage channels (1 is refused). privacy_level is always 2. A voice channel's event needs View Channel there. Covers (image) are image data (a data URI, up to 8 MB). recurrence_rule takes Discord's shapes (daily, with or without by_weekday; weekly or every other week on one day; monthly on the nth weekday; yearly on a date), worked out in UTC for bots; its start is ignored (the event's start is used), and the start must be one of its days. Ended and cancelled events are deleted; a repeating one moves on to its next occurrence. Somewhere-else events start and end on their own at their times; voice channel events are started by hand (status 2), cancelled if nobody starts them within 12 hours, and end once their channel has been empty for 5 minutes.
