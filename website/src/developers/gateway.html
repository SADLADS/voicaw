Connecting

GET /gateway/bot says where the gateway is (GET /gateway too, without a token): 

{ "url": "wss://api.voicaw.com/api/gateway", "shards": 1,
  "session_start_limit": { "total": 1000, "remaining": 1000, "reset_after": 86400000, "max_concurrency": 1 } }

Connect to it with the version and encoding, as on Discord:

wss://api.voicaw.com/api/gateway?v=10&encoding=json&compress=zlib-stream
  • encoding: only json. Anything else (etf) is closed with 4002.
  • compress: leave it out for plain text frames, or zlib-stream for one zlib stream across the connection, flushed after every payload (binary frames ending in 00 00 ff ff). What you send is never compressed.

The gateway then sends Hello with the heartbeat interval (41.25 seconds). Send a heartbeat that often (Voicaw closes a connection that hasn't sent one in about 67 seconds with 4009), and Identify within 30 seconds (or 4003).

The gateway never sends Reconnect (7) or asks for a heartbeat (1).

Ready and guilds

After Identify comes READY (the bot's user, its guilds as unavailable, the session_id and resume_gateway_url), then one GUILD_CREATE per guild (with the Guilds intent). A guild comes whole: its roles, emoji, the channels and active threads the bot can see, and every member (large is always false), with presences if you asked for the Guild Presences intent. voice_states is always empty.

Resuming

If the connection drops, the session waits two minutes for a Resume, keeping the last 1000 events. Resume on a new connection (at resume_gateway_url, the same address) and you get everything after seq, then RESUMED. Closing the connection yourself with 1000 or 1001 ends the session at once, as on Discord.

Intents and events

Intents choose which events you get. None is privileged: no approval is needed for any of them. Message content is included whether or not you ask for Message Content.

Guilds1 << 0GUILD_CREATE, GUILD_UPDATE, GUILD_DELETE, GUILD_ROLE_CREATE, GUILD_ROLE_UPDATE, GUILD_ROLE_DELETE, CHANNEL_CREATE, CHANNEL_UPDATE, CHANNEL_DELETE, CHANNEL_PINS_UPDATE, THREAD_CREATE, THREAD_UPDATE, THREAD_DELETE

Guild Message Typing1 << 11TYPING_START
Guild Scheduled Events1 << 16GUILD_SCHEDULED_EVENT_CREATE, GUILD_SCHEDULED_EVENT_UPDATE (also when one starts, ends with status 3, or moves on to its next occurrence), GUILD_SCHEDULED_EVENT_DELETE, GUILD_SCHEDULED_EVENT_USER_ADD, GUILD_SCHEDULED_EVENT_USER_REMOVE; with Guilds, guild_scheduled_events in GUILD_CREATE

Always, whatever the intents: READY, RESUMED, INTERACTION_CREATE (to one of the bot's sessions; see Commands and components), GUILD_MEMBERS_CHUNK, USER_UPDATE (for the bot itself) and GUILD_MEMBER_UPDATE for the bot's own member. Discord's other intents are accepted but bring nothing: Moderation, Integrations, Webhooks, Invites, Direct Messages (bots have no DMs), Auto Moderation and Polls.

Not sent: bulk deletes come as one MESSAGE_DELETE per message, and removing every reaction as one MESSAGE_REACTION_REMOVE per reaction. Bans have no event of their own (the member's GUILD_MEMBER_REMOVE comes).

Events only come for what the bot can see: messages in channels it has View Channel in, and presences and members of guilds it's in. A channel that becomes visible or hidden to the bot (a permission change) comes as CHANNEL_CREATE or CHANNEL_DELETE.

Limits and sharding

  • Up to 16 sessions at once per bot (more are closed with 4008).
  • Up to 120 payloads a minute sent by the bot, as on Discord (more: 4008).
  • shard: [id, count] is accepted, and guilds are split with Discord's formula, (guild_id >> 22) % count. Voicaw's ids are small numbers, not snowflakes, so in practice every guild lands on shard 0: one shard is all a bot needs.
