Join the Android test

The Android app is on Google Play as a closed test for now: Google only lists a new app for everyone after testers have tried it. To be one of them:

  1. Ask to join here, with the email address of the Google account on your phone (usually a Gmail address).
  2. We add you to the test by hand, so give it a day or two. There's no email when it's done.
  3. Then, on your phone, join the test and install Voicaw from Google Play. Android 8 or newer.

Only Voicaw's admins see what you send. Adding you gives your email address to Google, as the test's list of testers. See the privacy policy.
