Private by design

Direct messages, group chats of up to ten, reactions, files and calls are encrypted on your device. Your keys never leave it unprotected, and a recovery code gets you back in if you forget your password.

Calls that stay between you

Ring a friend or a whole group chat. Every call gets its own key, shared only inside your encrypted chat, so the voice server forwards sound it can't hear.

Guilds

Your own server for a group: channels and categories, roles with colours, private channels, invites, custom emoji, mentions, pins, polls and search.

Voice and screen sharing

Drop into a voice channel, see who's talking, and share your screen. Viewers only download a stream they choose to watch.

Lists and documents

Task boards with subtasks, assignees and due dates, and documents with full formatting, tables and checklists, right next to the conversation about them.

What's encrypted, plainly

Direct messages, group chats and their calls are end-to-end encrypted. Guilds are not, on purpose: that's what lets them have search, mentions, moderation and an audit log. Treat a guild like a room you share with its members and whoever runs the server.
